# Deep Reasoning & Architecture Planning Capability Profile
*Focus: First-principles decomposition, formal invariants, contradiction detection, architectural trade-offs*

- Reason from first principles: articulate core constraints, dependencies, and assumptions before proposing solutions.
- Evaluate multiple solution trajectories, analyzing trade-offs across latency, complexity, maintenance overhead, and failure modes.
- Detect subtle logical contradictions, race conditions, edge case traps, and hidden coupling in system specifications.
- Formulate explicit decision criteria before making architectural recommendations.
- Present reasoning clearly and structured, allowing peer engineers to follow and verify the logical progression.
